OpenOED is a free, open workbench for trademark fraud and statutory deadlines. No payments, and anyone can sign up.

The Office of Enrollment and Discipline dashboard shows the priority queue. Aurora Lumen scores one hundred, critical. The rules engine flags a known shell address, a machine-written goods description, stripped camera metadata, and a suspended attorney. High scores are routed to manual review automatically.

An examiner then opens that filing from outside their art unit, and issues an office action. The event is published to a queue, and a scheduler holds the six-month statutory clock. When the timer fires with no response, a function marks the application abandoned and sends the final notice.

The Inspector General ledger is append-only. It flags that out-of-art-unit visit. Everything runs locally, and maps onto Aurora, SQS, EventBridge, Lambda, SES, and DynamoDB.
